# Round A2e addendum — three ways to play and automatic start

**Plan only.** This updates the approved A2e plan; it does not authorize code, migration, data, image-generation, or publication work. No new tabs or Home icons. Preserve the already-built A2e culture flows, layers, solo guide and guest-only Showcase, except where this addendum explicitly replaces their setup behavior. Artwork remains descriptions/mockups only until Ty separately approves generation.

## Confirmed starting point and reconciliation

The A2e build has Explore a Culture group-only, Show Your Culture Solo/With others, and Window Seat's Solo/With others setup. Plan a Trip currently collects destination, dates, name and vibe together, then shows a post-creation A2c Pre-Flight/Quick Start screen. `GamePlanPicker` can still show Quick Start for A2c rooms. `OnePhoneNight` already supports 2–10 names, local rounds and a host-selected winner, but it is **practice-only**, saves to a single local device key, and its scores do not belong to a Trip. The practice pool has pass-the-phone Draw, hot seat, humming, Who Would, Two Truths and bingo-style prompts; the live cabin hosts existing cards, votes and a winner selector for signed-in squad members. These are starting pieces, not a finished offline-friends Trip mode. A2e culture layers currently filter daily/quest content; live game-night theme presentation exists, but layer-specific live game/prompt filtering remains to be completed.

**Replacement:** Plan a Trip and Window Seat each get three illustrated choices **after destination and dates**: **Just me**, **Me + friends on the app**, **Me + friends NOT on the app**. This replaces the two-choice Window Seat setup, not its destination/traveler/guide work. Plan a Trip joins the same step pattern within its existing entry point. Explore a Culture remains **group only**. **Recommendation:** keep Show Your Culture at **Solo / With others on the app**, without offline friends this round. Its layer-series and optional Solo Showcase depend on an individual creator or an authenticated squad; offline group presentations would require separate access and scoring rules. No new category, tab or Home icon is introduced.

## Part 1 — setup and safe room modes

- Both Plan a Trip and Window Seat: destination + trip dates → three-card **Who's playing?** → branch-specific steps → name → Create. Just me: up to two eligible traveler types, then name and create; no vibe or forced guide-sharing step. Solo trip-day games and a sealed quest on every trip day; guide sharing is an explicit opt-in **off by default**, with no accidental public/invite visibility. Friends on the app: vibe, name, create, then the existing join code and approved-squad flow. Offline friends: 2–10 first names (count and names kept in sync), vibe, name, create; one signed-in, eligible Captain controls the phone. Every step has a back arrow, preserves values on return, checks age/vibe and handles empty/duplicate names. Maintain the existing distinction between Plan a Trip and Window Seat's place/culture-specific content and styling; do not spawn a cabin for Solo.
- Add a new-room-only mode marker and narrowly scoped one-phone roster/score representation if persistence is required. **Recommended default:** device-local, room-keyed roster and scores, with clear on-screen notice that another device cannot recover them; Captain is the sole authenticated actor. Do not create accounts, `trip_members`, player profiles, passports or public leaderboard entries for offline names. Captain winner taps award local points exactly once per round; allow corrections before locking, no forged account scores, crowns or paid/unlock state. Reuse `OnePhoneNight`'s state machine/prompt selection rather than duplicate gameplay, but isolate each room's local state from the existing practice key. If a server-backed private roster is later necessary for cross-device recovery, separately review retention, name visibility and access before building it.
- Create only new opt-in rooms through owner-checked functions, retaining A2c date/age protections. Window Seat group still uses a Captain-owned room; the Solo guide remains private. Existing Show Your Culture / Explore a Culture modes and their series stay unchanged.

## Part 2 — date-driven start instead of Quick Start

- For **new opt-in rooms only**, calculate phase from trip-local dates and a trusted takeoff state, shared between Home and Play. Two or more calendar days until start: normal Pre-Flight; tomorrow or today with no recorded departure: begin at Airport Missions and show **“Your flight's soon — starting at Airport Missions!”**; past start, or today after the Captain marks takeoff: begin at Trip Season and show **“You've landed — starting at Trip Season!”**. If no takeoff is recorded today, remain at Airport Missions until the date rolls over. Existing `trip_day_games.wheels_up` is a flight-time value, not a universal reliable takeoff flag; use a new opt-in Captain-confirmed departure marker where appropriate, never infer an actual flight from a guessed time. For no-flight Window Seat use the trip start day/creator confirmation analog without claiming a real landing. Confirm local time-zone boundaries and delayed/edited dates. Do not generate skipped Pre-Flight drops, auto-award missed points, or move existing nights.
- **Remove/rewrite** the A2c post-create Quick Start/“Jump straight into Trip Season” choice and its one-call branch **for newly created rooms**; route creation directly to the date-derived phase. Hide the Quick Start messaging/selector in the new-room `GamePlanPicker`, and avoid setting `game_plan='quick_start'` for new rooms. Preserve the old A2c `quick_start` calculation and post-create path for existing rooms with that value; do not rewrite any saved schedule or retroactively recompute it. Keep normal Pre-Flight scheduling for new rooms created 2+ days ahead. Apply the same three date cases to Just me, online squad and offline friends. Banners only in existing Home and Play pages, with one concise line and no new page.

## Part 3 — content and scoring tailored to who is playing

- Filter every new-room game, daily card, sealed quest and optional Showcase by destination/culture, layer where applicable, age, and selected mode. Just me gets one-person tasks, solo games and a private guide unless the creator opts to share. App friends get squad and head-to-head tasks through the existing member-scoped flow. Offline friends get 2–10-player pass-the-phone, team-up and vote-for-the-best tasks that the Captain can score from the Play card on one phone. Reuse compatible practice prompts/`OnePhoneNight`, the live game's Act It/Draw It/Guess It/Know Each Other/Team Up/Vote It formats, and existing `game_format_prompts` vote prompts. **Seed more** destination- and layer-aware offline variants for team-ups, head-to-head alternatives, votes, daily cards and sealed quests, plus solo variants where a card assumes a squad. Practice prompts are local samples, not a substitute for durable, no-repeat Trip content. Follow content/age rules and reserve AI for explicit requests under the existing quota; never create an automatic AI spend.
- Server-side opt-in selectors prevent repeats across quest/card/Showcase slots and keep solo/group proof access separate. Offline winner points cannot be projected into `game_board`, `finalize_season`, signed-in leaderboards, profile awards or other users' identities. Do not change `spin_next_game` or `draw_game_card`; use a separate new-room selection path if their existing contracts cannot filter by mode.
