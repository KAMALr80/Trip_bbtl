# Round A2e — room-specific play and culture layers

**Plan only.** No app, database, or room data changes in this round. Nothing will be published without Ty's approval. Build in separately reviewed parts; artwork generation requires a separate approval.

## What exists today

- Travel From Home has two entry buttons, but both launch the same `CultureExperiences` home setup. That setup offers Solo and With others for both; it does not store whether the person chose Explore a Culture or Show Your Culture, and it does not ask for a culture layer. A separate At Home squad setup exists for three destination packs; it is not the same culture-experience flow.
- Window Seat uses the shared setup, which starts with “Who's playing?” rather than defaulting to Solo. It has seven traveler types (up to two), vibe, dates, 0–10 preparation days, a daily card plan, solo sealed quests, fare targets, streaks, stamps, and a photo/story guide. The screen and server currently limit the travel experience to **12 days**; preparation is stored but does not yet produce its own optional quest schedule. Group setup currently requires selecting an existing Trip.
- The solo mission pool has 30 templates; home daily cards have 14 templates and travel daily cards 12. AI planning is available only when the creator can spend an associated Trip's credits; otherwise the editable ready-made cards are the fallback. The current catalog has 15 named games and 170 card/wheel/vote format prompts, but neither the cards nor the game picker is filtered by a chosen culture layer.
- Existing `solo_quests` are readable by their owner only, and the solo experience has no Trip membership or group leaderboard. Existing `culture_exp_days` and private photo reads can include a creator's Trip members; the broader culture experience read can also show finished public experiences. Those are **not** safe as a Showcase guest feed. The current `culture_quest_sessions` code is for an unrelated Culture Quest game, not a viewer-only Showcase invitation.
- The shared `RoomExperienceCard` and room backgrounds already distinguish postcard/cabin from warm-paper/living-room styling. But setup and solo screens are shared, and the Game Night cabin and stage art are flight-themed. The profile currently shows culture badges/stamps, not a per-culture layer series. One existing From Home Solo experience exists in the database; it must remain usable as a legacy record.

## Part 1 — room choices, setup, and timing

- Distinguish **Explore a Culture**, **Show Your Culture**, and **Inspire Someone** at creation and in every downstream view. Explore a Culture is **With others only**; Show Your Culture keeps Solo/With others; Window Seat starts in Solo with an optional With others choice. Existing experiences retain their recorded mode and behavior; do not rewrite or silently convert the existing From Home Solo record.
- Window Seat Solo: destination → traveler types (up to two; Night owl only 21+) → trip start/end dates of any positive length → name → review/create/plan. Keep optional preparation up to ten days before the trip, with its own scheduled prep cards when chosen. Daily card plus one solo sealed quest on each trip day; destination/type-personalized, written for one person. Guide is the Showcase, with fare-class target, streak and bonus stamps; no group cabin, code, leaderboard or crowning. For long trips, expand the no-repeat pool and generate days in bounded batches rather than materializing an unlimited number at setup; date arithmetic and per-day limits must be tested.
- From Home group setup: own or selected culture → layer → 2–7 days → vibe/age-safe choices → optional halfway video-call Game Night when 4+ days → in-person finale format/location → name/review/create. Captain owns a **new A2c-marked room**, with that room's join code and approved squad; do not attach newcomers to an unrelated existing Trip. Explore a Culture uses the chosen destination culture and additionally offers “A bit of everything.” Show Your Culture explicitly identifies the Captain's own culture as the source for every player's cards and presentations. Reveal Showcase details on day 1; present and crown at the finale. Show Your Culture Solo follows the same culture/layer choice but creates a private guide without a scoring squad.
- Server changes: add an experience-kind marker and culture-layer selection to `culture_experiences` (nullable for legacy rows), bounded new-room creation functions that create the group Trip and experience together, and track/mode/date/layer/age validation in identity-checked private functions with authenticated-only wrappers. Expand Window Seat day constraints only for newly marked Solo experiences; preserve existing 2–7 day and legacy constraints. Reuse existing A2c timing, Captain/co-Captain and teen/Family enforcement rather than changing core game functions. Every update/delete is scoped by experience/Trip id plus owner or approved membership as appropriate.

## Part 2 — layers, content, and series

- Store eight Show Your Culture layers: Food & Drink, Music & Dance, Language & Sayings, Holidays & Traditions, History & Heroes, Fashion & Art, Games & Fun, Everyday Life. Explore adds “A bit of everything.” The selected culture and layer govern daily cards, sealed quests, the Showcase theme, and *which prompts and games are offered* at both Game Nights; the Captain's culture governs Show Your Culture group content. Use a server-enforced, per-experience no-repeat ledger across daily/quest/Showcase/game-night prompts, plus a reviewed fallback for exhausted pools. Keep positive, place-grounded “the why,” consent and safety rules; Family-only for minors and no adult-only content without existing approval. AI is opt-in through existing quota/credits only, never silently spent.
- Match existing game types without renaming stored catalog entries: Food & Drink → Taste It, Make It (Cook-Off), Guess It, Know It; Music & Dance → Sing It (Whose Song Is It), Move It, Guess It; Language & Sayings → Know It (Phrase of the Week), Guess It, Tell It; Holidays & Traditions → Know It, Make It, Tell It, Dress It; History & Heroes → Know It (Destination Trivia), Tell It, Draw It; Fashion & Art → Draw It (Gartic Phone Night), Make It, Dress It; Games & Fun → Act It, Team Up, Vote It, Know It; Everyday Life → Know Each Other, Tell It, Guess It, Find It. “A bit of everything” deliberately mixes the eight. Seed layer-tagged safe variants for `culture-pool` daily cards, `security_missions` quests/Showcase and `game_format_prompts`/catalog-linked night cards; new prompts are needed especially for language, history, fashion, games and everyday-life depth. Filter choices, not the legacy catalog or its saved labels.
- Series: store creator/culture/layer completion in an experience-scoped collection, award exactly one stamp per **distinct finished layer**, and expose completed parts in a series on the creator's profile. Server blocks repeating a finished layer for the same creator/culture unless they explicitly select “Do it again”; a replay remains visible but does not mint another unique-layer stamp. No backfill or changes to old rooms.
- Database: new layer-tagged seed rows and metadata, unique/idempotent collection rules, owner/member-scoped reads and authenticated grants/RLS on every new public table, functions for layer-aware selection and series reads. Keep direct selectors/private proofs inaccessible. Do not alter `game_board`, `finalize_season`, `spin_next_game` or `draw_game_card`.

## Part 3 — solo Showcase Night and safe guest viewing

- For **Show Your Culture Solo** only, after the run offer “Invite people to your Showcase.” Creator sets presentation date, time and place, shares an experience-scoped link/code, and can revoke it. A signed-in private-beta guest opens a *viewer-only* page containing only the creator-approved guide and presentation details; at the event they may watch, react once per account and vote once per account. Presenting earns a small **fixed, one-time** solo bonus, awarded by the server after the valid event begins. The exact bonus is a proposed **5 points**; Ty can adjust before building. No likes yet.
- Reuse existing share/invite UI and solo guide data, **not** the Trip join-code action: a Trip code would make the guest a squad member. Store a separate revocable, expiring Showcase viewer code/hash scoped to the experience, signed-in account and event; grant only a narrowly projected viewer read function (guide items explicitly approved by the creator, date/time/place, aggregate reactions/votes). Keep viewer identity in a separate invitation/viewer table, never `trip_members`, `culture_quest_players`, `solo_quests` or scoring tables. No INSERT/UPDATE access to quests, proofs, score or guide content; never return storage paths, private notes, squad roster, other people's rows or raw proof URLs. Use creator-approved guide images through a narrowly authorized read/signing path, not the existing broad `culture_days`, `culture_shared`, `can_view_culture` or photo bucket policy. Votes/reactions go through identity-checked, idempotent event functions and return only safe aggregates. One creator-only bonus ledger key prevents replay. Test guessed/expired/revoked codes and guests who also belong to another Trip.
- Database: viewer-code/event/invitation and reaction/vote tables (or narrowly scoped equivalent), explicit grants then RLS, unique one-vote/one-reaction constraints, creator/guest-checked RPCs and minimal projection. Never add guests to leaderboards, crowns, proof feeds or public browsing through a policy shortcut. Do not widen existing proof/photo permissions.
